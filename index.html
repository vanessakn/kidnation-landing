Your questions, answered.

Can I try KidNation for free?
Yes. KidNation includes a free first month so families can explore the app, meet the characters, and see which stories, songs, and games their kids love most.
What age is KidNation designed for?
KidNation is designed for kids ages 6 to 11, with content built to feel fun, safe, and engaging for both early and growing independent learners.
How will my child interact with KidNation?
Kids can tap, move, sing, speak, create, and play through stories and activities using touch, voice, camera play, and movement-based interactive moments.
Do I need multiple accounts?
No. One family subscription is designed to make it easy to get started, so parents can manage access while kids jump into their favorite characters and experiences.
What will my child learn with KidNation?
KidNation helps kids build confidence, curiosity, kindness, creativity, communication, and real-world problem-solving through music, movement, stories, and play.
